Copenhagen is a city best experienced, not just described. From the colorful waterfront of Nyhavn to cozy cafés, historic streets, and modern architecture, every corner has something unique to offer. Watch the video below and get a closer look at the places, atmosphere, and experiences that make Copenhagen an unforgettable destination.

Copenhagen is one of those cities that feels easy to explore from the very first day. Most of the main attractions are close to each other, making it perfect for walking or cycling. Whether you're exploring colorful Nyhavn, enjoying Danish food or discovering the city's modern architecture, there is always something new around the corner.

  • Explore the city by bike to save time and experience Copenhagen like a local.
  • Visit popular attractions early in the morning to avoid large crowds.
  • Try local Danish food and visit smaller cafés away from the main tourist areas.

Three days are enough to experience many of Copenhagen's highlights while still having time to slow down and enjoy the atmosphere. Plan your main activities in advance, but leave some room for spontaneous discoveries along the way.

Pro tips

  1. Get a Coppemhagen Card if you plan a visit several attractions and use public transportation regularly
  2. Bring a reusable water bottle and explore local bakeries and markets for affordable meals
